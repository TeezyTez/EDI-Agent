# CNIIN / iFreight Mapping Knowledge

genjb_job_number is the unique reference of the sender. In iFreight it often maps to JOB_NO or shipment job number.

cnict_container_number means container number. In iFreight it often maps to CTN_NO, CONTAINER_NO, or container main number.

genjbde_nad_postal_code means sender postal code. It often maps to POST_CODE, ZIP, or address postal code fields.

genjbde_nad_country_iso_code means sender country ISO code. It often maps to COUNTRY_CODE or CNTRY fields.

process_date means message processing date. It may map to CREATE_DATE, RELEASE_DATE, or business document date depending on context.

qref_custom_reference with value PICKUP means pickup reference. It may map to PICKUP_NO or pickup reference fields.
